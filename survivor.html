Survivor
In Identity V, Survivors work together as a team to decode cipher machines, avoid the Hunter, and escape through the exit gates. At the start of the match, your main goal is to find a cipher machine and begin decoding it. While decoding, pay attention to the calibration prompts and try to hit the correct area to avoid making noise that can reveal your location. If the Hunter finds you, run through the map and use obstacles such as pallets and windows to create distance. This is known as kiting, and learning when to drop pallets or vault windows can help you survive longer. If another Survivor is placed on a Rocket Chair, you can rescue them, but be careful because the Hunter may be nearby. Once enough cipher machines have been decoded, the exit gates become available. Survivors should then work together to open a gate and escape. Communication, teamwork, knowing your character's abilities, and understanding the map are all important for becoming a better Survivor.
