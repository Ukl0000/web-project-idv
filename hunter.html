Tutorial

Hunter
In Identity V, Hunters work alone to track down Survivors, interrupt their decoding progress, and prevent them from escaping through the exit gates. At the start of the match, your main goal is to quickly locate Survivors by checking nearby cipher machines and looking for clues around the map. Once you find a Survivor, chase them and use your Hunter's abilities to shorten the distance and land attacks. Survivors may use pallets, windows, and other obstacles to kite you, so learning to predict their movement and choose efficient routes can help you end chases faster. After knocking down a Survivor, place them on a Rocket Chair and watch for teammates attempting a rescue, but avoid spending too much time guarding one chair while the others continue decoding. As the match progresses, balance chasing, guarding, and applying pressure across the map. Good decision-making, efficient chasing, understanding each Survivor's abilities, and knowing when to switch targets are all important for becoming a better Hunter.
